Sidecove: Arc-style Spaces and Vertical Tabs for Chrome

SUMMARY (the manifest "description"; 132 characters at most)

Spaces, pinned tabs and favourites in Chrome's side panel, for people moving from Arc. Auto-archives idle tabs. Free, no tracking.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
DETAILED DESCRIPTION
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Missing Arc's sidebar? Sidecove brings its way of organising tabs to Chrome's side panel: spaces, pinned tabs and favourites, down the side of your browser.

Sidecove is for people moving from Arc to Chrome, and for anyone who wants vertical tabs and spaces without changing browser. It stays close to what Arc's spaces set out to do and adds to it where that helps.

SPACES
- Separate your tabs into spaces, such as Work, Home or a project. Each space has its own colour and icon, chosen from line symbols or emoji.
- The space bar shows every space as an icon, or as a name chip if you prefer names. Switch with a click, the keyboard, or your mouse's side buttons.
- Move a tab, favourite or folder to another space by dropping it on the space's icon.
- New tabs open in the space you are using, including links opened from other apps.

PINNED TABS AND FAVOURITES
- Each space keeps its own pinned tabs, favourites and nested folders (up to four levels deep).
- Favourites sit in a grid at the top of the space. An open favourite shows a minus button that closes the tab and keeps the favourite; a closed one opens in its own new tab.
- Hold one tab over a favourite for a moment and drop it to make a folder of the two.
- Pinned tabs and favourites are stored as ordinary Chrome bookmarks in a folder called "Sidecove", so they sync with your Google account through Chrome and you can see them in the bookmark manager.

KEEPS TABS TIDY
- Auto-archive closes tabs you have not used for a while (you choose how long, or turn it off). Favourites are never closed this way.
- Archived tabs are listed so you can open them again.
- Clean All closes a space's temporary tabs into the archive, with Undo.
- Search your open tabs, pinned tabs and favourites across every space (Cmd or Ctrl+F).
- Select several tabs with Cmd or Ctrl click and Shift click, then move, pin, archive, close or put them in a folder together.

KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
- Alt+S opens and closes the side panel.
- Alt+D pins or unpins the current tab.
- Alt+Shift+C copies the current tab's address.
- With the sidebar focused: Ctrl+1 to Ctrl+8 jump to a space, Ctrl+9 to the last one, Ctrl+Shift+[ and ] step through them.
- Shortcuts for next and previous space, next and previous tab in a space, and a numbered space can be set at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

HOW IT DIFFERS FROM ARC
- Sidecove is an extension, not a browser. You keep Chrome, your profile, your other extensions and your Google account.
- It lives in Chrome's side panel (Chrome 116 or later). Chrome's own tab strip stays where it is.
- It does not copy everything Arc does, and it cannot import data from Arc. If you already use Chrome tab groups, it can offer to turn them into spaces.

PRIVATE AND OPEN
- Sidecove sends nothing to any server: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. It reads your tabs' titles and addresses only to show and manage them in the sidebar. Pinned tabs and favourites sync only through Chrome's own bookmark sync.
